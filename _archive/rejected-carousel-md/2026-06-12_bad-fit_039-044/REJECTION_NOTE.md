## Rule Added

Future candidate scoring must include:

- Brand Fit: Is this a natural Munjanggun door, middle-door, ABS door, or free-measurement problem?
- Duplication Fit: Is this meaningfully different from existing carousel premises?
- Reality Fit: Is the causal chain credible in a real apartment?
